    What I Would Send The Rockets

    1. Keep two elite advantages on the floor.

    Durant and Sengun give Houston two half-court problem solvers. The optimizer rewards lineups where at least one of them is surrounded by three credible spacing or connective pieces, reducing late-clock stagnation.

    2. Treat Amen as a pressure multiplier, not a spacer.

    Amen's value comes from rim pressure, rebounding, passing, and defense. The best public-data lineups avoid pairing him with too many low-volume shooters, then let him attack tilted floors.

    3. Reed Sheppard changes the geometry.

    Sheppard's shooting, creation flashes, and positive BPM make him the cleanest public-data answer when the lineup needs more skill without giving back too much defensively.

    4. The next data request is possession-level proof.

    With internal data, I would validate these recommendations against actual lineup net rating, opponent shot quality, matchup assignments, transition frequency, and play-type outcomes after dead balls.
